Digital Planned Maintenance is a system used to manage machine maintenance schedule and task execution. The system creates maintenance schedule based on machine start hour or start week, the system also able to iterate the schedule of maintenance based on delta factor of machine's last execution, maintaining the maintenance schedule to be always align with the machine condition and prevent machine breakdown. 

![](/digipm-1.jpeg){.img-lg .mx-auto .rounded-lg}

The other feature of the system is about grading technician of the maintenance process, this feature allowing the supervisor to grade the quality of result and make sure the quality of the process is always meet the standard.

![](/digipm-2.jpeg){.img-lg .mx-auto .rounded-lg}

Digital Planned Maintenance occupied with a mobile app, it can be used by the technician to work with agile while doing the maintenance process, maintaining machine off time to be minimum and faster maintenance time.
