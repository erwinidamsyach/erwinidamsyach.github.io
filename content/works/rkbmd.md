In government administration, precision is non-negotiable. I developed a specialized management system for the Pemerintah Kota Batu to digitize the RKBMD (Rencana Kebutuhan Barang Milik Daerah) process.

![Page for input RKBMD data](/rkbmd-1.png){.img-lg .mx-auto .rounded-lg}

Before this solution, manual procurement planning was prone to human error—specifically in data entry and exceeding regulated budget quotas. My application introduces a fail-safe validation layer that cross-references every request against regional regulations in real-time. By enforcing strict quantity limits and standardized recording, the platform ensures that the city’s procurement is not only efficient but also 100% compliant with local laws, effectively eliminating administrative discrepancies
